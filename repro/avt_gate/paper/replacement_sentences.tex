% --- Whisper rule (method section, speech events) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Speech events.}
P01/P02 contains no speech, so speech events use a fixed Whisper rule instead of a calibrated threshold.
The audio check of a clip is reopened when Whisper hears a requested word of any speech event in the clip, or when FLAM scores a requested sound below $T_{\text{flam}}$.
The Judge then receives one text observation with the Whisper transcript (if a requested word was heard) and the FLAM lines below $T_{\text{flam}}$, and it may keep or revise the audio label of every event in the clip.
This rule does not depend on the initial PASS/FAIL label, and it is the same for every admission limit $\alpha$.
In Gate-only, a speech event is changed to PASS only when its own requested word is heard; otherwise its initial label is kept.

% --- Section 3.2: TC / TR prerequisites ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Temporal prerequisites.}
Temporal labels follow the event hierarchy.
A temporal-count (TC) label is scored per event, and only when that event passes its own prerequisites: A, V, and AVB for an audio event, and V for a silent event.
Failures of other events in the same video do not remove it.
A temporal-relation (TR) label is scored per video, and only when every event in the video passes A, V, and AVB.
These prerequisites are checked on both the human reference labels and the media-only judgment.
The human labels themselves are aggregated without prerequisites (majority vote per event for TC and per video for TR).

% --- Evaluation protocol (optional) --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Evaluation protocol.}
Prerequisites always use the media-only judgment; labels revised in the second stage are not propagated to other axes.
A unit without a model answer is reported as unanswered and is never counted as PASS or FAIL.
We report each method on its own answered units, on the examples answered by all compared methods, and on the fixed set of reference-eligible units with coverage.
